🧱 Breakout

Smash every brick to clear the wall!

Score: 0 Best: 0 Lives: ❤️❤️❤️

How to Play

Move the paddle with your mouse or the Left/Right arrow keys (touch-drag works too). Bounce the ball to smash all 3 rows of bricks. Each brick is +10 points. Miss the ball and you lose a life — you have 3. Clear every brick to win!
