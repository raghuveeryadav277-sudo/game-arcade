🐍 Snake

Eat, grow, don’t crash!

Score: 0 Best: 0

How to Play

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD — or tap the on-screen buttons on mobile. Eat the red food to grow and score. The snake gets faster every 5 foods. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game. Pick a speed below, then press Start.
