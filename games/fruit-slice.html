🍉 Fruit Slice

Slice the fruit, dodge the bombs!

Score: 0 Best: 0 Lives: ❤️❤️❤️

How to Play

Drag your mouse or swipe your finger across the fruit to slice it. Each fruit is +1 point — slice two or more in one swipe for a combo bonus! Never slice a bomb: it costs a life. You have 3 lives.
