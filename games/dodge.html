💨 Dodge Dash

Dodge the blocks — survive as long as you can!

Time: 0s Best: 0s

How to Play

Move your player with the Left/Right arrow keys — or drag your finger (or mouse) across the screen. Falling blocks get faster and denser the longer you survive. One hit ends the run. Your score is the seconds you survive!
