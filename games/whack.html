🔨 Whack-a-Mole

30 seconds. 9 holes. Whack fast!

Score: 0 Best: 0 Time: 30s

How to Play

Moles pop up out of the holes at random. Tap a mole to whack it: +1 point. But careful — tapping an empty hole costs you 1 point! You have 30 seconds. How many can you whack?
