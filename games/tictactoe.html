❌ Tic-Tac-Toe

Three in a row wins!

Your turn (X) Wins: 0

How to Play

Tap a square to place your mark. Get three in a row — across, down, or diagonally — to win. In vs Computer mode you play X and the computer plays O (it blocks and wins when it can!). In 2 Player mode, take turns on the same screen.

You Win!
